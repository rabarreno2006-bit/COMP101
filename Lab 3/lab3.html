Dogs and Cats

"Dogs and cats have so much to offer that it is useless to compare either."

Dogs are loyal and always happy to see you. They love walks and games. A dog makes every day feel exciting.

Cats are independent and full of personality. They love naps in the sun. A cat makes every day feel calm.

What they both give us

  • Someone to come home to
  • A reason to laugh every day
  • Comfort when life gets stressful
